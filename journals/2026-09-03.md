# Hair Health tips
1. Cleansing - regularly clean hair with shampoo 2-3 times a week
	- Shampoo type : 
		1. oily scalp - shampoo with exfoliating ingredients, salicylic acid, glycolique acid, tea tree oil
		2. Normal scalp - mild sulphate and paraben free shampoo
		3. Dandruff - ketoenazol
2. Conditioning - restore moisture
	- After shampoo squeeze out excess water and apply conditioner
3. Drying - gently pat and press hair, (microfibre cloth)
	- Blow drying - cold air
4. Oiling - after shower, apply 2-3 drops on hair not scalp
	- Never leave oil overnight
5. Nutrition - protein, healthy fats (nuts, seeds), vitamins a,c,e, biotin, minerals, hydration - 2 lts
