# Finding perfect hairstyle
## Facial Thirds
- Balance the gap from
- Hairline to eyebrows
- Eyebrows to bottom of nose
- Bottom of nose to chin
## Facial Symmetry
- The more asymmetrical the face, asymmetrical the hairstyle needs to be
- Only use symmetrical hairstyle if face is symmetrical
## Face Length
- The hair length should counter balance the face length
## Jaw Projection
- If jaw is developed and shaped good then short hair is good
