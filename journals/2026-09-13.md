# JAM #daily/jam
Mushoku Tensei launched a new episode where the protagonist meets the future version of himself. The future version tells the protagonist the reason which made his life miserable. The things he lost because of not being careful and trusting a being who is plotting the protagonist's ruin carefully. The future version offers advice and his journal to the protagonist and cries one last time at the thought of his loved ones and passes away. Protagonist then takes the body of the old man to burn the body and collect the remains and buries them. The protagonist promises himself to use the warnings and advice to carefully navigate through his live and protect his loved ones.
